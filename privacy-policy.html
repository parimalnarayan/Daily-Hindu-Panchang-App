Privacy Policy — Daily Hindu Panchang

Effective date: 18 August 2026

Daily Hindu Panchang is a Hindu Panchang utility designed to calculate and display daily Panchang information, including tithi, nakshatra, sunrise and sunset, muhurat, festivals and related calendar information.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application handles information.

1. Information used by the app

Location. The app may request device location when you choose the current-location feature. Location is used to calculate location-dependent Panchang timings such as sunrise, sunset and other local timings. Core Panchang calculations are designed to run locally and the app does not send precise GPS coordinates to a Panchang server.

Saved preferences. Limited preferences such as language and selected location may be stored locally on your device.

The app does not require an account, password or registration.

2. Firebase and Google services

The Android build includes optional Firebase SDKs for Analytics, Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Storage, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Remote Config, App Check and related infrastructure. These cloud features are activated only after the project's real Firebase configuration is provisioned.

If cloud services are enabled in a released version, Firebase may process technical information such as app instance identifiers, diagnostics, performance data, crash information and notification tokens according to the enabled Firebase products. These services are used to operate, secure, diagnose and improve the application.

Firebase and Google service processing is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms. The project does not intentionally upload precise device GPS coordinates to Firestore merely for Panchang calculation.

3. Notifications

The app may request Android notification permission so it can provide daily Panchang reminders and Firebase Cloud Messaging notifications. You can deny or later disable notifications through Android system settings.

4. Information we collect

The app is designed around local calculation and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, contacts, photographs, messages or unrelated personal information. If cloud-backed Firebase features are activated, the limited technical data described above may be processed by the configured Firebase services.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. If you use Android's Share feature, information you choose to share is passed to the Android sharing interface and the application or person you select.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local preferences can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Firebase-managed technical records are retained according to the configuration and retention controls of the corresponding Firebase services.

7. Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose informational utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use a local-first design to minimize transmission of personal information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when functionality, data handling or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.
