Privacy Policy — Daily Hindu Panchang

Effective date: 18 August 2026

Daily Hindu Panchang is a Hindu Panchang utility designed to calculate and display daily Panchang information, including tithi, nakshatra, sunrise and sunset, muhurat, festivals and related calendar information.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application handles information.

1. Information used by the app

Location. The app may request your device location when you choose to use the current-location feature. Location is used on the device to calculate location-dependent Panchang timings such as sunrise, sunset and other local timings. The app is designed to perform these calculations locally and does not send your precise GPS coordinates to a Panchang server.

Saved preferences. The app stores limited preferences locally on your device, such as language selection and the selected/saved location, so that the app can remember your choices.

The app does not require an account, password or registration.

2. Information we collect

The Daily Hindu Panchang application does not intentionally collect or maintain a remote database of users' personal information. Panchang calculations are performed locally whenever possible.

The app does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, contacts, photographs, messages or other unrelated personal information.

3. Third-party services

The Android application may use Google Play services for obtaining device location when you explicitly grant the relevant Android location permission. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

The app does not use a third-party Panchang API for its core calculations.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Because the app is designed around local calculation and local storage, the developer does not receive your precise location merely because you use the Panchang calculation features.

If you use Android's Share feature, the information you choose to share is passed to the Android sharing interface and the application or person you select. That sharing is controlled by you and is subject to the recipient's privacy practices.

5. Data retention and deletion

Preferences and saved location information are stored locally on your device. You can remove this information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the application, subject to normal Android behavior.

6. Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose informational utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

We use a local-first design to minimize transmission of personal information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's functionality, data handling or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
